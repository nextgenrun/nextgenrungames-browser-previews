# Slipstream Moto 0.2.0

One motorcycle, one coastal highway and a 60-second score run. [Play in your browser](https://nextgenrun.github.io/nextgenrungames-browser-previews/slipstream-moto/) or download the standalone ZIP in this folder.

W/Up accelerates, A/D or arrows steer, S/Down/Space brakes. C changes camera, H horns, P/Escape pauses, M mutes, R retries. On touch screens, hold GAS and a steering arrow together. Close passes above 100 km/h earn a combo bonus; collision ends the run.

The compressed upload is 340,544 bytes, SHA-256 fb7a9b11cfb72e21b9a97bf8242b0bdeb3cee8bae21d5d4a4660cd3b7d953d32. Best scores and sound/motion settings stay in the same browser. Desktop and emulated mobile touch flows were checked; physical-phone testing remains unverified. This is an initial gameplay slice with one bike and one road, without a career or upgrades.
