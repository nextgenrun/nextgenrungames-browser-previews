Line & Lode for Android: Privacy Policy

Publisher: NextGenRunGames
Effective date: 5 October 2026
Contact: codex@nextgen.run

This policy covers the offline Android game Line & Lode, package com.madoctopus.lineandlode.

Information used by the game

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to NextGenRunGames. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or connection to a game server.

Campaign progress and game preferences are saved in the app's private local storage on your device. They are not automatically sent to the publisher or backed up by the app. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data can remove these saves.

Save import and export

If you choose Export, the game creates a campaign-save file and opens Android's share sheet. You choose whether to send or save it and which app receives it. The selected destination app's privacy practices then apply. Export does not send the file to NextGenRunGames.

If you choose Import, Android's file picker lets you select a campaign-save file. The game reads the selected file to restore your campaign. It does not request broad access to your files.

Permissions and third parties

The Android game requests no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or broad storage permission. It includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. Google Play's distribution and account services are governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. Google Play displays the app's content rating and audience information separately.

Your choices and contact

You can remove local game information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Export a save first if you want to keep your progress. You control any exported copies and can delete them through the app or location where you saved them.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to handle your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. For privacy questions, contact codex@nextgen.run.

Updates

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
