## Controls

**Spirestorm:** A/D, arrow keys or mouse movement steer. Hold Space or the left mouse button to dive, hit an enemy and release to rebound. F or the right mouse button brakes. Q/E strike sideways; W or the middle mouse button strikes upward. 3 uses the starting jump. Escape pauses; M toggles sound.

**Flingkeep:** hold an enemy with the pointer, move to lift or swing, then release to throw. Space grips or drops the hovered enemy. Q/W/E cast unlocked spells at the pointer. Escape pauses; F toggles fullscreen; H shows details.

## Content and credits

Both games contain stylized or fantasy violence, blood and dismemberment.

Development used AI-assisted code, game text and artwork. Spirestorm artwork was generated for the project; music was generated with Suno and reused from the studio owner's existing Dig Game library. Its project and audio credits are included in the archive, including source attribution for third-party sound effects and ambience. Flingkeep includes stock TTS voices generated in advance, with voice provenance in `voices/README.md`.

Credits and applicable third-party terms remain with the packaged material. No blanket open-source licence is added by this repository.
