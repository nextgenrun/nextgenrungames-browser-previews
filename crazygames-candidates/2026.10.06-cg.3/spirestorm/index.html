KNOW WHAT GOES BUMP IN THE SPIRE

A knight’s field notes.

Mint diamonds mark breakable props; a glowing root returns a pulse. Pale dashed rings mark copies, while the solid gold marker marks the real guardian. Returned projectiles carry a mint trail; hostile shots have coral outlines. Get above your target. Watch for attack flashes. Guarded enemies open up between defenses. W or middle mouse strikes upward: a clean hit adds lift and briefly lets you pass through that enemy. Empty upward swings and guarded hits add no lift. Q and E share a 0.42-second recovery. A dive that misses every enemy rebounds strongly and brings the lava closer; missing when the fire is already too close ends the run. Release dive before trying again. Optional side rings reverse a fall strongly upward, then accelerate without stopping combat. Hold the brake to stay with enemies. Enemies can drop a rare heart: collect it to restore one life. Height, supply bells and boss victories give no automatic healing. Combat earns lift progress: qualifying returns advance one stop automatically. Lava stays paused below an active boss encounter.
