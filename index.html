Controls

A/D, arrow keys or mouse movement steer. Hold Space or the left mouse button to dive, hit an enemy and release to rebound. F or the right mouse button brakes. Q/E strike sideways; W or the middle mouse button strikes upward. 3 uses the starting jump. Escape pauses; M toggles sound.
